Frog Loop

Mobile game
iOS · Android
2026

A memory and mental-math game. A frog climbs an invisible minefield; the mines never move, so every mistake shows a bit more of the safe path. Designed, built and published solo, with AI writing the code.

App StoreGoogle Play

Phaser 3 · Vite · Capacitor · Firebase · AdMob · RevenueCat · Google Analytics 4 · Claude Code

The idea

A short-session game that trains memory instead of testing luck. The field never changes, so the player literally learns the way up. In the math worlds every row is a problem, and the right answer is the safe cell.

My role

Everything except typing the code: game and UX design, art direction, product decisions, testing on real devices, store listings and releases. I set tasks for Claude Code, checked the result on the device and iterated.

What’s inside

  • 395 levels plus endless modes
  • Math worlds: multiplication, digit sums, powers, Fibonacci, primes, digits of π
  • Custom levels from any word, date or number — a way to memorise a phone number or a PIN
  • Daily challenge, skins and accessories, world and country leaderboards
  • 9 languages, offline play, cloud saves with Apple or Google sign-in

How it’s built

  • One web codebase (Phaser 3) packaged for iOS and Android with Capacitor
  • Firebase for sign-in, cloud saves and leaderboards
  • AdMob and RevenueCat for ads and the one-time purchase
  • A token-based design system for all 29 popups and my own dev tools to tune them live
  • Google Analytics 4: I read the funnel and plan updates from it

Let’s Scale

Mobile game
iOS · Android
2026

How long is a pirate ship next to a car? How big is Jupiter next to Earth? Two silhouettes, one with a known size: resize the other until it looks right and see how close you got.

App StoreGoogle Play

Unity 6 · Firebase · AdMob · RevenueCat · 10 languages · Claude Code

The idea

A game about a sense of scale — the thing an architect trains every day. Accuracy is measured in proportions, not metres: being off by half costs the same for a mouse and for a whale.

My role

Concept, game design, visual design and releases on both stores. My second game, on a different engine: the same way of working moved from a web stack to Unity.

What’s inside

  • Worlds: animals, countries and islands, space, transport, architecture
  • Daily challenge: five new pairs a day, the same for every player, with streaks
  • Four mini-games, including a duel with a friend by code
  • A collection: objects become copper, silver, gold or platinum cards
  • World and country leaderboards, Game Center

How I work on it

  • A design “studio”: every visual decision — colours, cards, icons, sounds, texts — is made on a page of options that I review and pick from
  • Hundreds of silhouettes made in a vector pipeline with AI and checked by hand
  • Store pages and texts in 10 languages

Vase-mode slicer with loops

Grasshopper · Rhino
3D printing

My own slicer for floor lamps: it cuts a model into one continuous spiral path and adds small loops along the line, so the printed shell gets texture and lets light through.

Grasshopper · Rhino · G-code · FDM printing

The task

Standard slicers print a vase as a smooth wall. I wanted a lamp shade with a woven, looped surface, which no slicer can do out of the box.

What I built

A Grasshopper definition that takes any form, slices it for vase mode and generates loops along the toolpath. Every parameter is a slider: how far a loop leaves the contour, how much plastic is extruded, loop frequency and width.

Status

Prototype, paused. I wrote it with hints from ChatGPT; today, with Claude Code writing whole parts of the logic, it’s the first project I want to bring back.

Logo made from 7,000 coffee capsules

Strauss · Tel Aviv
Studio Punkt project

A script turned a logo into a numbered assembly map; a CNC machine cut and engraved the base; the wall was assembled by hand.

Rhino · Grasshopper · CNC · manual assembly

The task

For a renovation at Strauss’s capsule coffee factory we decided to build the logo wall out of real coffee capsules — about 7,000 of them.

What I built

A script that converts the image into a pixel grid, groups it by capsule colour and assigns a number to every colour. The same file drove the CNC: slots for the capsules were cut and the numbers engraved, so each capsule had its exact place.

Result

From a distance it reads as a logo, up close it is a rhythmic surface of thousands of objects.

The Grasshopper definition: image → pixels → colour number for each capsule.

Modular 3D-printed lamps

Personal project
In progress

One set of printed parts that assembles into a pendant, a floor lamp or a table lamp.

3D printing · LED · modular system · prototyping

The idea

Modules that change form, height, colour combination and use. The pendant is the simplest version: printed elements that lock together and build volume through rhythm, folds and translucency.

Engineering

The floor lamp with a built-in LED strip needed real problem-solving: joints between parts, stability, how light passes through the material, mounting, wiring and assembly — each solved through printed prototypes.

Porcelain lamp for Punkt

Tel Aviv
From 3D model to porcelain

Dozens of printed prototypes, then a plaster mould and a porcelain cast whose relief glows from inside.

3D modelling · 3D printing · plaster mould · porcelain casting

Process

I iterated the form digitally and printed dozens of prototypes until the final object: a rounded lamp with a relief that reads like a map or a city plan. Then I made the plaster mould and cast it in porcelain.

Result

The porcelain is thin enough that the pattern changes with light: by day a surface relief, in the evening a glowing inner structure.

Apartment hunt map

Personal tool
Google Sheets · Apps Script · Leaflet

I send a link to a listing; AI reads it and fills a row in a Google Sheet, and the apartment appears on a map with tram and train lines, walking times and everything we need to decide.

Claude Code · Google Sheets · Apps Script · Leaflet · OpenStreetMap · Google Calendar · WhatsApp

How it works

  • A listing link (Yad2, Facebook, a screenshot from a chat) becomes a row: rent, monthly costs, rooms, floor, shelter, parking, contact
  • The sheet is the only source; the map reads it and writes statuses back
  • Tram and train lines with walking time to the nearest station along real streets

One card per apartment

  • A status shared with my partner: new, waiting for reply, viewing booked, viewed, good fit
  • Book a viewing: it lands in both our Google Calendars with reminders
  • A ready first message to the owner, sent to WhatsApp in one click
  • Links to the listing and Google Maps

Plugins for Illustrator and 3ds Max

Studio workflow
Built with AI

Small tools for routine tasks in my architecture work: areas and dimensions, automatic numbering, export for 3D printing.

Adobe CEP · ExtendScript · MAXScript · Claude Code

Area and bounding box (Illustrator)

A panel that shows the area and dimensions of the selection and updates live with every click. Choice of units; selects several objects and sums their areas — what you need for plans and specs.

Auto numbering (Illustrator)

Numbers text objects automatically, for drawings and presentation sheets.

Groups to STL (3ds Max)

Saves every selected group of models as a separate STL file, ready for the 3D printer — instead of exporting them one by one.
