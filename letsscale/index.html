Privacy Policy for Let's Scale

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Artem Svirinovskyi (“we”, “us”) handles information in the mobile game Let's Scale (the “Game”), available on the Apple App Store and Google Play. By using the Game you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

The Game is playable without creating an account. Some data is collected automatically to run the leaderboards, and some only if you choose to sign in.

DataWhenWhy
Anonymous user ID Automatically at first launch Identifies your device for the leaderboards without any personal details.
Email address & name Only if you sign in with Apple or Google To create and manage your account and sync your progress across devices. (Apple “Hide My Email” is supported.)
Game progress (levels, collection, awards) Only if you sign in Saved in the cloud so you can continue on another device.
Nickname and country (optional) Only if you enter them in your profile Shown next to your result in the global, per-country, daily and weekly leaderboards. Without a nickname you appear as “Player N”. Offensive nicknames are filtered out.
Score, daily-set result (percent and points), player number During play To rank players in the leaderboards and award daily and weekly prizes.

Stored only on your device: your settings, your profile photo, and your game progress if you do not sign in. The photo never leaves your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

The current version of the Game contains no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases and does not use your advertising identifier. If this changes in a future version, we will update this policy before the change is released.

2. How we use information

  • Run the leaderboards: global, per-country, daily and weekly.
  • Create and manage your optional account and save your progress in the cloud.
  • Prevent cheating and abuse, and keep the service secure.

3. Third-party services

We share data only with the service provider needed to run the leaderboards:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, cloud database) — Privacy & Security

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep account, progress and leaderboard data while you use the Game. Daily and weekly results are kept for a limited time for the leaderboards and prizes. You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data directly in the Game: open your profile → Delete account. This removes your leaderboard entry, your daily and weekly results and your cloud save from our systems, and revokes Sign in with Apple.

You may also request deletion by contacting us at support@vias.im and we will delete your data.

5. Security

All data transmitted between the Game and our service provider is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS.

6. Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

7. International users

Our service provider may process and store data on servers located outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, use the in-game account deletion or contact us at the email below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date above and, for significant changes, provide notice within the Game or store listing.
